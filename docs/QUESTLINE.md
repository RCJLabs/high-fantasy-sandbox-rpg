## Main questline: The Draining

### 1. Wrong Things
*Start depends on your background (see the table at the end), and every start leads to Maren's
waystation on the north road.*
- Maren mentions small wrong things: the wisps at the stones are dimming, and a hunter found
  a Greyfang wolf **"hollow"** (alive, but grey and empty-eyed).
- Find the hollow wolf near Greyfang Hollow. Put it down or leave it. On it: a burnt
  **rune-mark** nobody recognises.
- Brannoc, the Riverwatch scout, knows one person who might: whoever studies the stone circle.
- **Teaches:** the region, the compass and map, the first fight, that something is wrong.

### 2. The Waking Stones *(existing quest, reframed)*
- The crystal at the stone circle is waking early because something is **pulling magic
  through it**. Find the three rune shards around the circle.
- The existing choice stays (give the shards to Riverwatch, return them to the stones, or keep
  them). Whatever you choose, the crystal shows a **drain-line**: a thread of light running
  north into the Thornwood.
- **New:** at night under the dark Wanderer, wisps gather at the circle and fade as you watch.

### 3. Ask the Forest
- The drain-line leads into the Thornwood, where the outlaws live. The garrison won't go in.
- **New:** the outlaw camp (the Thornwood Free) and its leader *(name to choose)*. They've lost
  people too: a hedge-mage of theirs came back hollow, like the wolf.
- They'll only talk once you prove yourself. Either:
  - deal with the **bandit camp** that's been raiding everyone (the existing "Thieves by the
    Fire", whose bandits turn out to be paid in strange coin with the same rune-mark), or
  - bring them supplies from Riverwatch (a garrison reputation cost).
- Either way, the outlaws' scouts have seen hooded people going in and out of **the Old Barrow**.

### 4. The Barrow
- The existing dungeon: pressure plates, swinging blades, the lever puzzle, and the
  **Stone Guardian** (an ancient warden, older than any of this, and not on anyone's side).
- **New:** past the vault, a hidden working chamber: empty wisp cages, drained creatures in
  jars of light, and notes in a cultist's hand. They're harvesting life to "feed the first of
  them". Where it's kept is marked on a map.
- **Choice that echoes later:** free the last caged wisp (it follows you and lights the way),
  or take its essence (a powerful but corrupting reward, and the outlaws hear about it).

### 5. What They Made
- At the place on the map, deep in the Thornwood at night: a small ritual site, cultists, and
  **the creature**. It's the demo's final boss: a beast swollen with stolen life, magic seeping
  out of it, half-finished and unstable *(new: needs a model, see "Costs")*.
- Kill it. As it dies, the stolen light leaves it in a stream, flowing north.
- **Cliffhanger:** a cultist's letter on the altar: *"The first is a failure. The others are
  ready. Send the harvest to…"* and a map with a dozen marks across Vaelora. The demo ends with
  Riverwatch, the outlaws and Maren reacting, depending on what you did, and a closing line
  setting up the full game.
